01 Project Identity

Research Question
How can we re-engineer the physical infrastructure of AI data centers — addressing hardware circularity, water depletion, and energy consumption — to shift from "Red AI" to "Green AI"?
Territory: Green AI Data Centers Outcomes: Interactive web · Data diagram · Game

Curiosity Statement

By investigating the massive energy demands of data centers, I aim to map out a tactical shift toward green infrastructure, proving that sustainable system design is not just a requirement for human and environmental health, but a fundamental necessity for corporate survival.

Why It Matters

AI integration is inevitable, but its physical toll is unsustainable and hidden. Data centers consume 70–80% of withdrawn water through cooling. AI chips last 1–3 years and defy recycling. By 2030, 50 million metric tons of e-waste annually. A tactical shift is a survival imperative — not just an ethical choice.

Key Conceptual Shifts
Water Consumption vs Withdrawal

70–80% of cooling water is consumed, not returned. Evaporated water becomes greenhouse gas — a negative feedback loop where data centers contribute to the warming that forces more water use.

Framing Green AI vs Sustainable AI

Green AI: energy, water, carbon. Sustainable AI: community impact, algorithmic fairness, governance. The distinction reframed the inquiry beyond just efficiency.

Hardware Embodied Carbon

Even with carbon-free energy, the embodied carbon in chips (copper, cobalt, rare earths, 1–3 year lifespan) remains massive. 50M tons e-waste by 2030 — this shifted focus to the Digital Product Passport and hardware circularity.

07 Tools & Learnings
Tool NotebookLM

Deep research adds 20–30 credible sources per session. Good for overviews, but AI sometimes misspells or misphrases — requires 2–3 clarification rounds. Quiz and flashcard features useful for synthesis. Best used as a thinking partner, not a research replacement.

Tool Gemini Pro

Prompt specificity directly reduces hallucination — vague prompts cost 2–3x more iterations. Paid account gives free image generation. Hex color codes enable precise visual control. Generating study plans works, but tracking real vs predicted progress reveals gaps.

Tool Flow (Veo)

10s video at 30 credits each. Image → animation pipeline works well. Risograph and specific art styles are achievable. Character creation has a ~15-iteration breaking point. Credit strategy: free for images, reserve for video.
